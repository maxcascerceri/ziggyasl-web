title: How to Learn Sign Language Online for Free
---

You can get surprisingly far in ASL without spending anything. The trick is knowing which free resources to use for what: a dictionary for looking things up, guides for the basics, and real people for practice.

## Free resources on ziggyasl.com

- **[ASL dictionary](/dictionary):** 700+ signs and phrases on real video, with notes on how each sign is made
- **[Beginner guides](/learn):** the alphabet, numbers, greetings, phrases, colors, family, feelings, and more
- **[Sign your name](/learn/how-to-sign-your-name):** type any name and watch it fingerspelled, letter by letter
- **[Blog](/blog):** plain-language answers to beginner questions, from grammar to Deaf etiquette

Every sign is filmed with a real signer, and our team includes certified ASL interpreters, who sign our videos and check our signs. No account, no sign-up.

## Other free options

- **SignSchool:** free lessons, grammar notes, and a dictionary
- **Lifeprint (ASL University):** free ASL lessons organized like a college course
- **Your public library or community center:** some host free or low-cost ASL classes
- **Deaf community events and ASL meetups:** the best free practice there is; go as a respectful guest

## A free 2-week plan

1. **Days 1–3:** learn the [alphabet](/learn/asl-alphabet) and fingerspell your name
2. **Days 4–7:** [greetings](/learn/asl-greetings) and [basic phrases](/learn/basic-asl-phrases)
3. **Days 8–10:** [numbers](/learn/asl-numbers) and [question words](/learn/asl-question-words)
4. **Days 11–14:** [pronouns](/learn/asl-pronouns) and [feelings](/learn/asl-feelings); introduce yourself in sign every day

## When free stops being enough

Free resources are great for looking things up. What they usually don't give you is structure: a daily plan, review at the right moment, and a reason to come back tomorrow. That's what an app is for. Ziggy is a subscription with a free trial, so you can see if it fits before you pay. If you're comparing options, see [the best apps to learn sign language](/best-asl-apps).
